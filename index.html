Five things to know before you spend a rupee.

Shedding is hairs falling out. Thinning is hairs growing back finer and shorter than before. Stress, fever, crash diets and illness can cause heavy shedding that fully recovers in about four months.
  • Shedding at the same thickness is usually temporary
  • Less shedding but wispier hair on top is miniaturisation, and that's the one that ends in baldness
Pull a fallen hair and compare it to one from the back of your head. That test tells you more than any product label.
Testosterone converts to DHT. DHT binds to receptors on follicles at the top of the scalp, and only there, because only those follicles carry the sensitive gene. Each cycle the follicle shrinks.
  • The sides and back never go — same DHT, different receptors
  • An oil on the skin can't change an instruction coming from inside
  • Stop treatment and the loss resumes where it left off
Starting an effective treatment pushes out weak resting hairs to make room for stronger ones. That's the dread shed. It usually begins between week 2 and week 8.
  • Nothing visible before month 3
  • Real thickness changes around month 6
  • Honest verdict at month 12
You see your head every day, so you can't see gradual change.
  • Same spot, same light, same time, hair dry, twice a week
  • Three angles: hairline down, crown from above, side profile
  • Flash off, never wet — wet hair exaggerates in both directions
Every before/after on this page exists because someone did this for months.
Hair supplements are sold as food, not medicine, so nobody checks what's in the bottle. Independent testing has repeatedly found lead, mercury and arsenic in ayurvedic and herbal products. "Proprietary blend" on the label means the company never has to tell you the dose of anything. And the animal-derived fillers you'll find in men's "vitality" formulas — bull testicle extract, placenta extract, deer antler velvet — are in there because they sound potent, not because they do anything for hair.

Meanwhile the boring stuff genuinely moves the needle:
  • Protein — hair is keratin. Chronically under-eating protein thins it.
  • Ferritin — low iron stores are one of the most common causes of shedding in India, especially if you're vegetarian
  • Vitamin D3, B12 and zinc — cheap to test, cheap to fix
  • Crash diets and gym cuts show up as shedding 2–3 months later, not immediately
Food and a ₹600 blood test will do more than any capsule with Sanskrit on the box. It won't beat your genetics on its own — nothing you eat will — but deficiency-driven shedding is very common, and it is completely reversible.
